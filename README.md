### 👋 Hi, I'm Danilo Fróes!
### Electronic & Computer Engineering and Mathematics Student at UFRJ
Welcome to my GitHub! I am an engineer student passionate about **Robotics**, **Embedded Systems**, and **Artificial Intelligence**, constantly seeking to bridge the gap between hardware and intelligent algorithms.

- 🤖 **Competitive Robotics:** Former electronics and programming member of MinervaBots, UFRJ's robotics competition team, where I worked on the Mini Sumo (500g), Mega Sumo (3kg) and Trekking robots. I led the Mini Sumo (500g) project twice and served as the team's Programming Coordinator, taking part in **Zé Pequeno**, world champion at the All Japan Robot Sumo Tournament 2024, and in the RoboChallenge 2025 title. Although I'm no longer on the team, I keep competing as an independent hobby with my own team, **Lótus**.
- 🏭 **Technological Researcher (PIBITI/CNPq)** at the Laboratory of Simulation and Optimization of Productive Systems (LASOS), National Institute of Technology (INT). I worked on **LasosLibrary**, a library for automating the testing and comparison of machine learning models, and currently work on intelligent algorithms for production scheduling in **See The Future**, a discrete-event simulator.
- 🏥 **Scientific Researcher** in the Jovens Cientistas Cariocas 2025 program (Rio de Janeiro City Hall), developing **SALVE**, a project that uses machine learning to predict infectious disease outbreaks and support crisis management in public health. The project was also selected in the City Hall's **Bora Fazer 2026** call to continue its development.
- 🎭 **Electronics & Robotics Project Engineer** at **Theatrum**, building projects for Rio's Carnival and theater productions that combine 3D printing, electronics and robotics.
- 🎮 Technician in Digital Game Programming through the technical high school program at Firjan SESI/SENAI.
